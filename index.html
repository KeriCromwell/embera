Easy to get started

Start watching in three steps.

1

Download and extract

Download the Windows x64 ZIP package from GitHub Releases. Extract the files to a folder.

2

Open the app

Run Embera.exe from the extracted folder. Choose your language and appearance in settings.

3

Add your source

Enter your M3U URL or Xtream Codes credentials. Once your list loads, pick something and press play.

A few things to know

Before you download.

Are any channels included?

No. Embera is a player only. It does not provide channels, movies, series or IPTV subscriptions. Add your own source that you are authorized to access.

Do I need to add my source every time?

No. Your sources and channel lists are saved on your computer. The app loads your last-used source at startup, and you can refresh the list whenever you need.

Will the TV guide and content details work with every source?

Availability depends on the data supplied by your source. EPG needs a compatible TV guide source, and the amount of content information varies by provider.

Where can I report a problem?

Open an issue on GitHub ↗. Include the app version and steps to reproduce the problem. Keep source URLs, usernames and passwords out of public reports.
